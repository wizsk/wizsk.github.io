Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this app handles information when you use it to view Salah (prayer) times.

1. Information We Collect

The app itself does not collect, store, or sell personal information.

The app does not require you to create an account and does not collect information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or personal files.

2. Prayer Times API

The app uses a third-party API to obtain and display Salah times.

When the app requests prayer times, information necessary for the API to provide those times may be sent to the API provider. Depending on how the API works, this may include information such as a location, coordinates, date, or other parameters required to calculate prayer times.

The app does not independently collect or retain this information.

The third-party API provider may collect, process, or retain information according to its own privacy policy and terms. Please review the API provider's privacy policy for details about its data practices.

3. Data Storage

The app does not maintain its own servers or databases for collecting personal information.

Any information handled by the third-party API is subject to that provider's policies, not this app's data storage practices.

4. Third-Party Services

The app relies on a third-party API to provide Salah times. Because this service is operated independently, its data collection and processing practices are governed by its own privacy policy.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties.

The app communicates with the third-party API only as necessary to retrieve Salah times.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how the app handles information, you may contact the app developer through the contact information provided on the app's store listing.
